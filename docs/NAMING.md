## 4. Provisional Decision & Rationale

**Selected Provisional Winner:** **Storefront Desk**

### Rationale:
1. **Operator Command Center ("Desk"):** Preserves continuity with the operator-in-the-loop philosophy (first-send approvals, bounded negotiation, immutable audit trail). It is a "desk" where an agency or operator works with AI agents, not an unguided bot.
2. **SMB Resonance ("Storefront"):** A storefront is the primary identity of any North American local business—whether a coffee shop on Queen West in Toronto, an auto repair shop in Chicago, or a boutique bakery in Austin.
3. **Immediate Judge Comprehension:** Within 5 seconds, a hackathon judge understands what the product creates: digital storefronts and outreach for businesses that need them.
4. **Clean Distinction from Mock Illusions:** Unlike static mockups, Storefront Desk builds and hosts *real* responsive storefronts backed by verified evidence.

### Boundaries & Limitations:
- The filesystem directory and remote Git repository remain unchanged (`placeholder-1` and `Zen-cronic/change-order-desk`) per operator instructions.
- Marked as **provisional** in metadata and user-facing documentation until final operator branding review.
